For my Distributed Quantum Computing class's final project, I set up a QAOA (Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm) based solver for the Bin Packing Problem. This problem is NP-Hard, so the goal was to create a quantum approximation algorithm for it, and study its performance. I ran a noise ablation study to measure the effects of Depolarizing error and Thermal Relaxation error on the QAOA solver.
