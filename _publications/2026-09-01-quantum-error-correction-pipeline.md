I am currently developing an end to end quantum error correction pipeline, which will accept a quantum circuit and stabilizer code, and it will produce a fault tolerant quantum circuit with the provided code applied to it. I plan on achieving this by utilizing the symplectic representation of stabilizer generators. I am also creating a paper detailing the implemented algorithms and proving related theorems.
